# First real-model results: v0.10 on a Colab A100 (14 October 2026)

The evidence is in `evidence/v0.10.0-colab-a100-20261014/`:

- the full exported zip;
- `summary.md` and `experiment.log`;
- per-run `report.md` files.

## Setup

| item | value |
|---|---|
| model | Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, revision `aa8e7253…`, bf16, pure greedy decoding |
| hardware | NVIDIA A100-SXM4-80GB, batch size 16, transformers 4.57.6 |
| design | 3 independent task plans (seeds 2026-2028) × 2 arms |
| levels | 3 per run: 12 discovery and 24 validation tasks each |
| final test | 48 held-out final tasks per plan |
| run time | about 4 minutes per run (543-639 calls) |

The two arms differ only in their starting strategy:

- **self arm:** the plain default, "Solve each task carefully. Return only
  the requested answer."
- **control arm:** a hand-written "Think step by step…" prompt.

The preflight passed and no run aborted. 99% of answers used the
`ANSWER:` line, and 98-100% of forecasts were valid percentages.

## Results (144 pooled held-out tasks per comparison)

| comparison | accuracy | wins/losses | p | Brier (paired valid forecasts) | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| self arm: selected vs starting policy | 65% → 70% | 9/1 | 0.011 | 0.328 → 0.198 | < 0.001 |
| self-improved policy vs hand-written prompt | 73% → 70% | 9/13 | 0.86 | 0.393 → 0.199 | < 0.001 |
| control arm: selected vs hand-written prompt | 73% → 73% | 0/0 | 1.00 | 0.393 → 0.231 | < 0.001 |

### What the evidence shows

1. **Self-model self-improvement works and transfers.**
   - In all six runs, the agent's rewritten self-model forecast its own
     success better on held-out tasks than its starting self-model, and the
     change was committed.
   - Example self-model: "multiplication: about 3 in 10 correct (2 of 8 so
     far)".
2. **Strategy self-improvement happened once.**
   - In seed 2026, the agent wrote itself a rule, "For multiplication tasks,
     include a detailed verification step … verify the multiplication
     process".
   - It passed validation (4 wins, 1 loss) and transferred. Held-out
     accuracy rose from 60% to 77% (9 wins, 1 loss, p ≈ 0.011).
   - Held-out multiplication went from **2 of 8 to 8 of 8**.
   - In the other two plans no strategy change passed the gate.
3. **It did not beat a hand-written step-by-step prompt** (70% vs 73%), and
   the loop could not improve that prompt's accuracy.

### Why strategy improvement was rare: the 3B model is a poor rewriter

- **Whole-strategy rewrites mostly made accuracy worse.**
  - Of 18 `repair` proposals, 11 lowered validation accuracy (accuracy
    regression).
  - In the control arm, 8 of 9 did. Those rewrites dropped the working
    "Think step by step" instruction and replaced it with instructions tied
    to specific tasks ("Count the 'i's in 'quizzical'"). One of them
    measured 0 wins and 9 losses.
- **Rewrites copied their context.** Proposals contained
  `=== DISCOVERY RESULTS ===` blocks, `Task:` lines, `ANSWER: Thursday`, and
  "verify against the reference answer", which is meaningless at test time.
- **Strategies mixed in forecasting instructions** ("adjust forecasts for
  multiplication to 30-40%").
- **Self-model truncation.** 4 of 18 recalibrations were discarded as
  truncated: after writing the per-type lines, the model kept copying
  evidence until the token limit.
- **Word reversal stayed at 0%.** This is a genuine inability ("attention" →
  "tnetieta"), not a scoring problem.

## Changes in v0.11, driven by this evidence

- **`add_rule` and `add_rule_2` (new defaults).** Instead of rewriting the
  whole strategy, the agent adds **one rule** for its weakest (or
  second-weakest) task type, and the harness appends it to the current
  strategy. What already works is kept, and each change is small enough to
  test. The proposer gets a focused context of about 1.3k characters instead
  of about 12k: that task type, its missed discovery examples with correct
  answers and the agent's working, rules already tried, and the actor's view.
  This mirrors the one successful case, which was a single added rule.
- **Screens on rules.** A rule that mentions forecasts or confidence, copies
  harness sections, or refers to correct answers is ineligible
  (`rule_off_lever`). A whole-strategy rewrite that copies harness sections is
  ineligible too (`strategy_echoes_context`).
- **Structured self-model.** Only the `<task type>: …` lines of a
  recalibration are kept, so trailing copied evidence or truncation no longer
  discards a good self-model.
- **Four levels.** Accepted rules accumulate, and the notebook budget is
  1100 calls per run.

## Claim boundary

Self-model calibration improved, and transferred, through the agent's own
recursive self-examination. Strategy accuracy improved by self-revision in one
of three plans. That is genuine but limited self-improvement of an
inference-time policy, for one 3B model and these task types. It is not
general self-improvement, the weights did not change, and nothing here
measures awareness.
